# Demo narration script

Each top-level `- ` bullet below is rendered as one spoken segment (in order)
by `generate_voiceover.py`. Edit freely — keep sentences short for natural TTS.
Voice: Gemini `Charon`, `en-IN`, warm Indian-English narrator style.

- Generating a Title Search Report for Gujarat land usually means logging into the government's AnyRoR portal, solving a captcha, downloading the record in Gujarati, and reading it line by line. It takes hours. Here is our agent doing it in minutes.
- I enter just four things — the district, the taluka, the village, and the survey number — and click Generate.
- Behind the scenes, the agent opens the AnyRoR portal, solves the captcha automatically, and pulls the official Record of Rights — the very same document a clerk would download by hand.
- Then Claude reads the raw Gujarati record and extracts every entry — sales, inheritances, partitions, and loans — while tracking which ones are certified.
- Out comes a clean Title Search Report: the full chain of ownership, a findings table, and any active encumbrance — like a loan with no matching release — flagged automatically. From four inputs to a finished report, in minutes.
